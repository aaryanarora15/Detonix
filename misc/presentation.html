HACKTRONICS 2026
The Problem

Emergency response can't wait on guesswork

Industrial fires and tank failures escalate in minutes. The tools most facilities rely on today weren't built to keep pace with a live incident.

Seconds matter

Responders need blast and thermal reach estimated before conditions change — static setback tables can't keep up with a live incident.

Wind

A real hazard zone isn't a circle. Wind speed and direction reshape the danger envelope, but most tools never account for it.

Predicting the hazard's change

Tools are employed to know about the burn pool's change and then BLEVE impact

High stakes, low margin

A wrong call at the incident perimeter puts crews in the same danger they were sent to respond to.

Our Solution

One physics-grounded platform, epoch till end

A live consequence-modeling and response console — not a static lookup table.

Cited physics, not guesswork

Point-source thermal radiation + TNT-equivalency blast modeling, in the style of CCPS Guidelines for Consequence Analysis and the TNO Yellow Book.

Real hazard shapes, on a real map

Wind-skewed thermal and blast polygons rendered live on satellite imagery — elongated downwind, compressed upwind.

Live or manual weather

Pulls current wind from Open-Meteo, or accepts a manual override for tabletop exercises and pre-incident planning.
